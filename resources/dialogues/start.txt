[Narrator]
(MC stands in the open doorway of his home.
It is night.
The hall is dark and cold. He sees the tall, still outline of a figure standing inside room.)

[MC]
(whispering, uncertain)
Kaelen?

[Narrator]
(The Brother, Kaelen, turns his head slightly. His armor is dull and stained. At his feet, a pale shape is visible on the stone floor.)

[Narrator]
(His gaze drops to the floor beside Kaelen.)
(A sudden, sharp intake of breath.)

[MC]
Elara...
(He rushes forward, ignoring Kaelen, collapsing next to his wife’s body. He touches her face—it is already cold.)

[MC]
(a desperate, broken sound)
No. Not her. You can't...
She was everything.

[Narrator]
(Kaelen's voice is unnaturally smooth, a sound that doesn't fit the familiar brother.)

[Kaelen]
She waited for the knight who never came home.
Three years I waited in the dark. You were always too late, brother.


[Narrator]
(He scrambles back, tears and anguish distorting his features.)
(A scream of pure rage.)

[MC]
I will kill you for this!
After all this time, this is how you return?!


[Kaelen]
(He offers a cold, almost bored smile.)
You cannot protect the living. You certainly cannot touch the damned.
Not anymore.

[Narrator]
(Kaelen’s form dissolves. Not a step, not a noise—just a quick, dark ripple, and he is gone. The air where he stood feels suddenly empty and colder.)


[Narrator]
(He falls forward, his voice reduced to a dry rasp.)
(A raw, defeated whisper.)

[MC]
Gone...
I let him escape.

[Narrator]
(He stays kneeling beside his wife.)

[Narrator]
(The only witness to his failure is the cold stone and the rain outside.)

[Narrator]
(As the storm rages, memories flood back, haunting and relentless.)

[Narrator]
(Shadows of the past loom large, whispering secrets long buried.)
